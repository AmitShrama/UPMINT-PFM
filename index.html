Upmint PFM — Proof of Concept

Personal Finance Management application · Feature & Status Document
Version: POC 1.0 Platform: Mobile (iOS reference build) Date: June 2026

1. Purpose

This document summarises the Upmint Personal Finance Management (PFM) proof of concept. It lists the features built across the application and records the current development status of each module. The objective is to make clear which parts of the PFM are functionally complete and which parts are still under active development.

The headline position is straightforward: the bank-data layer of the PFM is complete. All functionality that depends on aggregated bank account data — account linking, balances, transactions, consent management and the resulting analytics — is built and working. The Stocks and Mutual Funds modules are still under development.

4. Onboarding & Access

The user enters the PFM and is guided to connect their financial data. Account linking is the primary path; the user may also proceed without immediately linking a bank, since other account types (such as stocks and investments) may apply.

  • Branded launch / splash screen.
  • Welcome screen with a clear "Link Your Bank Account" call to action and a "Skip for now" option.
  • Secure session entry; logout returns the user to the welcome screen.

5. Bank Account Aggregation Complete

Bank accounts are linked through the Account Aggregator (AA) framework. The complete consent-driven linking journey is built:

  1. Initiate linking from the dashboard ("Link More") or the welcome screen.
  2. Mobile number / identity verification.
  3. OTP verification with auto-fill, resend and timer support.
  4. Account discovery across the AA network.
  5. Selection of one or more accounts (multiple banks supported).
  6. Consent review — purpose, data accessed, fetch frequency and validity shown before approval.
  7. Secure sync; linked accounts appear immediately on the dashboard.

Supported reference banks in the POC include HDFC, ICICI, Axis, State Bank of India, Kotak, IDFC First, Yes Bank, PNB, Bank of Baroda and Federal Bank.

Consent Re-use

Consent approval is requested only the first time a new account is added. Already-consented accounts are re-linked without repeating the approval step.

6. Accounts, Balances & Consent Complete

  • Total bank balance across all linked accounts.
  • Automatic background synchronisation with a "last updated" indicator — no manual refresh required.
  • Per-account cards showing bank, masked account number, balance and live sync status.
  • Balance privacy toggle (show / hide amounts).
  • Bank Details view per account — consent ID, Account Aggregator, data provider, purpose, fetch frequency, granted and valid-until dates.
  • Consent control — Revoke consent pauses data sync; consent can be reactivated.

7. Transactions & Categorisation Complete

  • Unified transaction history across linked accounts, most recent first.
  • Filters by type — All, UPI, Income, and by category.
  • Transaction detail view — name, amount, category, date & time, source account, reference number, payment mode and status.
  • Expense categorisation — the category of any transaction can be edited and is reflected across the summary.
  • Transactions are grouped by category within the accounts summary.

8. My Accounts Summary & Analytics Complete

A consolidated financial breakdown derived entirely from linked bank data.

  • Summary card on the Accounts dashboard with two filters — account-wise (All Accounts or a specific bank) and time period (Last Month, This Month, Last 3 Months).
  • Three views — Incoming, Expenses and Investments.
  • Full summary screen with category donut, top categories with proportions, a month selector, and category-wise transaction listing.
  • Top credits and top debits for the last 30 days.
  • Spending analytics — monthly spend, savings rate, category breakdown and weekly UPI trend.

9. Cards, Loans & Credit Score Complete

  • Credit cards — outstanding dues, limit and utilisation, due dates.
  • Loans & EMIs — outstanding amount, monthly EMI, rate and tenure; recurring EMI auto-detection from bank data.
  • Credit score (CIBIL) — score gauge, band, month-on-month change and contributing factors.

10. Notifications & Settings Complete

  • Categorised notifications — Payments, Recurring, Bills, Security and Insights — with unread indicators and "mark all read".
  • Notification preferences per category in the profile.
  • Profile — linked accounts, credit score entry, cards, privacy and data sharing, refer & earn, help & support.
  • Appearance — dark / light theme; in-app personalisation controls.

11. Stocks In Development

The Stocks module presents equity holdings and extends the linking pattern to depositories. Screens are built; data linking is under active development.

  • Stocks dashboard — total stock value, linked accounts count and per-holding rows (value, last traded price, quantity).
  • Holding detail ("Fund Details") — current value, quantity, last trade price, editable buy price, profit / loss (absolute and percentage), market capitalisation, P/E ratio, and pros / cons.
  • Add Stocks — depository linking via NSDL and CDSL, with OTP verification and holding selection.

12. Mutual Funds (MF) In Development

The Mutual Funds module presents folios and portfolio allocation, with linking via the registrars. Screens are built; data linking is under active development.

  • MF dashboard — total value, returns, invested amount and XIRR; allocation by category (Flexi Cap, Large Cap, Small Cap, Index, Hybrid, Gold).
  • Fund list with per-fund value, category, SIP amount and returns.
  • Fixed and recurring deposits (FD / RD) view.
  • Link Mutual Funds — folio import via CAMS, KFintech and MF Central, with OTP verification and folio selection.

13. Development Status & Next Steps

Complete (bank level). Account aggregation, balances, transactions, consent management, accounts summary and analytics are built end-to-end and operate on aggregated bank data.

In development. Stocks (NSDL / CDSL) and Mutual Funds (CAMS / KFintech) re-use the same consent-and-link pattern proven at the bank level. Their screens and flows exist; the remaining work is live data integration with the depositories and registrars.

Next steps.

  • Complete depository data integration for Stocks (NSDL / CDSL).
  • Complete registrar data integration for Mutual Funds (CAMS / KFintech / MF Central).
  • Unify Stocks and MF holdings into the overall net-worth and analytics views once data is live.
